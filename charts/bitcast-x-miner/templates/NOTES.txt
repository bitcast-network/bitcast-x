Bitcast X miner ({{ .Values.mode }}) for netuid {{ .Values.network.netuid }} on {{ .Values.network.name }}.

It advertises http://{{ .Values.publicIP }}:{{ .Values.port }} on chain; validators dial exactly that.
The pod turns Ready only after that advertisement is finalized:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deploy/{{ include "bitcast-x-miner.fullname" . }}

Before any restart or upgrade, take a consistent backup (operator runbook):

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec deploy/{{ include "bitcast-x-miner.fullname" . }} -- bitcast-x state-info
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec deploy/{{ include "bitcast-x-miner.fullname" . }} -- \
    bitcast-x backup-state --output /var/lib/bitcast-x/backups/<timestamp>
